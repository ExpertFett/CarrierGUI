Notes

Section dividers are 1px #3a3e3e. Section labels are #9ab (muted blue-gray), uppercase, ~11px. Buttons are flat #383c3c rectangles with 1px #6a6e6e border. Alert buttons (WAVE OFF, RESET CAM, FOUL DECK) are tinted red; affirm buttons (CUT, CLEAR DECK) are tinted green. The bottom of every tab has a ship-status strip and an event/bridge-status strip in a muted color so they don't compete with the controls above. This is the look we'd port the v1.3 5-tab layout into if we want to keep the existing visual continuity.
